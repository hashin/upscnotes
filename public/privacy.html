Privacy

Short version: your notes live in your browser and in your own Google Drive. This app has no server that stores them.

What stays on your device

Every note you write is saved in this browser (IndexedDB). If you never sign in, nothing you write ever leaves your device.

Google sign-in

Signing in with Google gives the app your name, email address and profile picture, used only to label your workspace and your public page. We never receive your Google password.

Google Drive

The app uses the drive.file scope, which lets it see and manage only the files it creates — a folder called UPSC Notes containing your notes as Markdown files. It cannot read anything else in your Drive. Notes are written straight from your browser to your Drive and do not pass through any server run by this project. Revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Publishing

If you choose a username and mark a note “Public”, that note file (and any images in it) is set to “anyone with the link can view” in your Drive, and a small record — your username, display name, bio, and a link to your public notes list — is stored so that upscnotes.hashin.me/your-name can find them. Unpublish to reverse it. Nothing is published unless you turn it on.

What we don’t do

No analytics or tracking cookies. No selling or sharing of data. No note content on our infrastructure.
